SOLARA BAY

An open-world crime game · a city of your own

You are Marlo Vane, fresh out of the county lockup with $120, a borrowed pistol and a debt to Ray Delgado. Solara Bay is sun, salt air and bad decisions. Steal a car. Run the jobs. Stay ahead of the Bay PD.

Explore the city on foot or behind the wheel, steal cars, outrun the police as your wanted level climbs, and work through Ray’s story jobs — deliveries, a dockside shootout and a getaway. Cash pickups and taxi fares pay along the way. Progress saves to this browser.

  • 1. Keys to the City — steal a car, reach Ray’s garage
  • 2. Sunset Delivery — run a package across town in 90 seconds
  • 3. Dockside Trouble — clear the rival crew at the docks
  • 4. The Long Way Home — lose a 3-star wanted level, reach the hideout
  • WASD Move / drive
  • Mouse Drag to look
  • E Enter / exit car
  • Click Shoot (on foot)
  • Shift Sprint
  • Space Jump / handbrake
  • M Mute   P Pause
  • R Restart mission
  • C Radio station   L Headlights
  • J Courier run (free roam)
  • Gamepad Stick moves, A jumps, X enters car

Original game: its city, people, cars and story are invented for Solara Bay. Nothing here is from any commercial game.
